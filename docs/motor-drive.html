Try the controls and see how each direction changes the six pin signals.

Connect your ESP32

  1. Choose your driver and download its firmware for the original ESP32 DevKit V1.
  2. Use the wiring guide in the ZIP, then verify and upload the sketch in Arduino IDE.
  3. In RoboSynct Android, open Dashboard → Options → Add motor widget. Pair the motor board over Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi.

Live motor controls and pin readings are in the Android app.
